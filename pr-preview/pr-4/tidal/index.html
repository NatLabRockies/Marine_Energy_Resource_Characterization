    Tidal Energy Datasets¶

    High-resolution tidal current datasets for U.S. coastal locations, generated using the Finite Volume Community Ocean Model (FVCOM).

    Available Datasets¶

    Dataset Locations Duration Resolution Grid Points
    High Resolution Hindcast 5 U.S. coastal sites 1 year each Hourly / Half-hourly 231K–1.7M per site

    High Resolution Hindcast¶

    The WPTO High Resolution Tidal Hindcast provides 3D tidal current data at five strategically selected U.S. coastal locations. The dataset includes depth-averaged current speed, power density, water depth, tidal range, and other engineering variables derived from FVCOM simulations.

    Location Period Sampling Grid Points
    Aleutian Islands, AK 2010–2011 Hourly 797,978
    Cook Inlet, AK 2005 Hourly 392,002
    Piscataqua River, NH 2007 Half-hourly 292,927
    Puget Sound, WA 2015 Half-hourly 1,734,765
    Western Passage, ME 2017 Half-hourly 231,208
